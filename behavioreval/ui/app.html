Set up
Once per machine: your key, the viewer login, who you are, your Chrome. Then share tests with the team from here.

  

    
Is everything ready?

    
My Chrome
Runs happen in a second Chrome started from a copy of the profile you use for dev (sign-in included). Your everyday Chrome keeps running. Pick the profile signed in to dev, start it, and leave that window alone during runs.

      

  

  
Your account

    
Your dev account email and the Chrome profile signed in to it are what matter. The facts are optional: fill them in if you want the simulated user to answer as you (where you live, your car…); leave them empty and it answers plausibly. Keep real third parties out.

    

      

      

        
        
        

    

    

  

  
Share with the team
Tests and profiles live in this folder, a copy of the team repository. Get what others added, or send yours. Put your tests in a set named after you or the topic (anderson_shopping) so files never collide.
