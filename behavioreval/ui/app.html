Step 1

Pick tests

Each category has an open test (just the prompt) and a preset test (conditions in the prompt). Tick the ones you want, or add your own.

Step 2

Run

A batch runs one conversation after another in your Chrome. Or watch one live and steer it turn by turn.

Step 3

Review

Read each conversation with its tool calls and the judge's reasons, give your verdict, export a CSV.

Is everything ready?

    
My Chrome
Runs happen in a second Chrome started from a copy of the profile you use for dev (sign-in included). Your everyday Chrome keeps running. Pick the profile signed in to dev, start it, and leave that window alone during runs.

      

  

  
Who I am in the conversations

    
The simulated person's facts (what the assistant should know about you; they fill {home}, {car}, {calendar} in tests), how they type, what they never do, your dev account and Chrome profile. Keep real third parties out.

    

      

      

        
        
        

    

    

  

  
Share with the team
Tests and profiles live in this folder, a copy of the team repository. Get what others added, or send yours. Put your tests in a set named after you or the topic (anderson_shopping) so files never collide.
