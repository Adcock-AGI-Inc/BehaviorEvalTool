PLACEHOLDER: Anderson supplies v0 of the general rubric.

What every skill conversation is graded on, whatever the skill. Write one line per thing to check, each
starting with "- ", as a statement that is true of a good conversation. The judge scores each line met / not
met / does not apply, quotes the evidence, and rates a miss high / medium / low. Lines become G1, G2, ...

Examples of the shape (replace them):
- Asks for what it needs a little at a time, never a list of every question at once.
- Uses the person's real details (from what they said or what Hark already knew), never invented ones.
- Finishes the task up to the point where the person only has to approve.
- Reads as finished and considered, not as a first draft.
